**Conclusion.** Design deficiency. No branch protection is configured on
`main`: the Settings > Branches page states that classic branch protections
have not been configured, and no protection rule or required check therefore
stands between any push and `main`. The control as designed does not achieve
its objective, because it does not exist. Scope limitation: classic branch
protections were inspected; repository rulesets were not inspected in this
test. Result feeds Finding 2.
